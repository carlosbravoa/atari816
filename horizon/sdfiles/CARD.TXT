Atari816: the SD card

Unpack everything here onto an empty SD card formatted as FAT32, keeping the folders.

Two files are not included, because they are Atari's and may not be given away. Put them
in the root of the card yourself:

  ATARIXL.ROM   16384 bytes   the XL/XE operating system
  BASIC.ROM      8192 bytes   Atari BASIC

Without them Horizon still starts; the classic Atari (F12, "Run in Atari", disk images,
cartridges, executables) does not.

Folders:
  /SYSTEM    Horizon itself and its system programs
  /BIN       the programs of the Programs menu
  /Music     songs for the Music Player
  /docs      Welcome.md: start here
  /programs  examples for BASIC and Sheet
  atari.ini  the classic Atari's settings (Settings > Classic Atari edits it)

The board needs the bitstream and cold.bin of the same release.
