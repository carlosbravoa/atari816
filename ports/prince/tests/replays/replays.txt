Test replays for the Prince of Persia port, recorded with upstream SDLPoP (prince-ref,
headless) from the scripted input in the .txt next to each .p1r. 12 ticks a second.
Recorded with:  rec.sh NAME.txt NAME.p1r "LEVEL seed=1"
(seed=1 is passed to prince-ref as "seed=1"; the .p1r stores the seed. It only matters for
fight: the guard AI uses the random generator. The level-1 runs give the same kid trace
with any seed.)
Every replay was checked with "prince-ref validate": the per-tick kid/guard state of the
validate run is identical to the recording run, at every tick.

Room numbers are the level's room numbers (as in the ref: status lines).
